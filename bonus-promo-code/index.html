BetOnGame Wagering Requirements Explained

The bonus percentage tells you how much promotional credit you get. The wagering requirement tells you how much qualifying betting you have to do before that bonus can potentially be withdrawn.

For the reviewed sportsbook offer, it's a 5× wagering requirement applied to the bonus amount, combined with conditions on qualifying accumulator bets.

Example: How a 5× Requirement Works

Take a ৳1,000 welcome bonus on an eligible account. Under the reported rules, the calculation would be:

৳1,000 × 5 = ৳5,000 in qualifying stakes

At the maximum advertised bonus:

৳11,000 × 5 = ৳55,000 in qualifying stakes

That figure is turnover: the total you have to stake. It's neither money you're bound to lose nor money you'll get to withdraw.

This is worth getting straight, because wagering carries risk. You could lose bets along the way, and reaching the required turnover still doesn't guarantee a profit.

Which Sports Bets Count?

Only accumulators count under the reviewed promotion. You need three selections or more, and none of them can be priced below 1.40.

In an accumulator, several selections ride on one bet. For it to pay out, all the required selections generally have to win. So under the reported rules, a single bet may not count toward bonus wagering, and neither may an accumulator with too few selections or odds that are too low.
