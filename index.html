Football Betting Markets

Pick a football fixture and you may find several markets attached to it, each about a different outcome.

First up is Match Result (1X2): home win, draw or away win, settled within the specified settlement period. Over/Under Goals depends on how many qualifying goals are scored in total. Both Teams to Score only needs each side to find the net once. With handicap markets, a stated advantage or disadvantage is factored in when the result is calculated.

Two bets on the same match won’t necessarily settle the same way. An Over 2.5 Goals pick needs a third qualifying goal, whoever scores it. A Match Result pick only cares about the final outcome. Check the settlement period as well, particularly if extra time or other match-specific conditions could come into play.

Live Betting

Once a match is underway, it moves into the live betting section and the markets start reacting to play. A goal or a red card in football can shift the odds, and so can a wicket in cricket. Sometimes a selection is suspended for a moment while the prices catch up.

That’s why the price on screen when you open a selection isn’t guaranteed to be the price you get when you submit it. On mobile, connection delays can slow screen updates and make the gap more noticeable. Think of a live market as a changing offer, not a fixed price that holds for the whole match.
